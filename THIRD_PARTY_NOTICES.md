# Third-party notices

## OpenCC dictionaries

Toolbox includes six unmodified dictionaries from OpenCC `ver.1.1.9`, by
BYVoid and contributors, under Apache License 2.0. Their license and attribution
are retained in `Toolbox/SubtitleTool/OpenCC/` and included in the application
resources. Toolbox uses an independent Swift conversion implementation.

Source: https://github.com/BYVoid/OpenCC/tree/ver.1.1.9/data/dictionary

## FFmpeg

FFmpeg is not bundled, copied into the DMG, or linked into Toolbox. Video tools
invoke the user's separately installed Homebrew executable. That installation
has its own configuration and licensing terms.
